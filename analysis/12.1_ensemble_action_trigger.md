# 12.1 — Action Trigger: Ensemble Hindcast

Return-period analysis for the ensemble-based action trigger using hindcast
damage estimates for the four target regions (Regions II, III, V, VIII).

---

## Setup

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Data source | Ensemble hindcast — `ensemble_500increments_corrected/` |
| Record | 2006–2024 (19 years, 79 storms) |
| Damage thresholds | 500–40,000 houses (500-house increments, 80 levels) |
| Probability threshold | **95 % only** (unanimous 12/12 ensemble members; 95%-only design adopted July 2026) |
| Regions | Region II (Cagayan Valley), III (Central Luzon), V (Bicol), VIII (Eastern Visayas) |
| Target RP (action alone) | 4.0 years (5 activations; Weibull: (19+1)/5) |
| Target RP (combined) | ≥ 3.3 years — obs-rain trigger (≥ 350 mm) adds 2014, giving 6 seasons → (19+1)/6 = 3.33 yr ✓ |

---

## Trigger logic

**Winner-takes-all region assignment**: each storm is attributed to the single target region
where its expected damage is highest (sum of ensemble exceedance probabilities across all
thresholds). A storm can only activate its primary region, preventing double-counting across
regions.

A region activates in a season when the ensemble probability of exceeding its damage threshold
is ≥ 0.95 (i.e., all 12 ensemble members agree). The trigger fires when any one of the four
primary regions activates.

**Primary region distribution** (79 storms): Region II — 41, Region III — 20,
Region VIII — 10, Region V — 8.

---

## Threshold calibration

Thresholds are found by **greedy lexicographic search** starting from the COMBOS baseline
values and adjusting upward only. The following constraints were enforced during the sweep
(multi-level constraints are retained for reference even though only 95% is active):

1. **Within-prob ordering**: T(R II) ≥ T(R III) ≥ T(R V) ≥ T(R VIII) at each probability level
2. **Cross-prob spacing**: each probability level must be ≥ 3,000 houses above the previous
   level for the same region (T80 ≥ T95 + 3k, T70 ≥ T80 + 3k, etc.)
3. **Upward-only from COMBOS**: thresholds can only increase from the COMBOS starting values

Greedy priority order: 95 % across all regions first, then 80 %, 70 %, 60 %, 50 %.

**Return period formula**: Weibull — RP = (RECORD_YEARS + 1) / n, applied consistently
for both storm-based and seasonal counting.

## Calibrated thresholds

### 95%-only design (ACTIVE — July 2026)

| Prob | Region II | Region III | Region V | Region VIII |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **95 % ← ACTIVE** | **6,500** | **5,500** | **3,500** | **3,500** |

Action trigger alone: **5 storms** → RP = (19+1)/5 = **4.0 yr**

5 activated seasons: Melor 2009 (RV), Haiyan 2013 (RVIII), Man-yi 2018 (RII, data quality concern — see note below), Molave 2020 (RV), Rai 2021 (RVIII).

Combined with obs-rain ≥ 350 mm (adds Hagupit 2014): **6 seasons → RP 3.33 yr ✓**

> **Note — Man-yi 2018 data quality concern**: The ensemble fires for Man-yi (Nov 2018, 2 cycles,
> min_dist=131 km), not Mangkhut/Ompong (Sept 2018, 13 cycles, min_dist=4.5 km). Mangkhut
> errored in ensemble processing (prob=0.0 in all regions). The 2018 season is defensible by
> impact (Mangkhut: 2.6M; Yutu: direct hit at 2 km) but ensemble provenance is weak. Fix
> required: reprocess Mangkhut 2018 in the external ensemble aggregation script.

### Reference thresholds (80–50%, retained but NOT active in current design)

| Prob | Region II | Region III | Region V | Region VIII |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 95 % | 6,500 | 5,500 | 3,500 | 3,500 |
| 80 % | 11,500 | 9,500 | 9,500 | 8,000 |
| 70 % | 18,000 | 15,000 | 12,500 | 11,000 |
| 60 % | 25,000 | 20,000 | 17,000 | 14,000 |
| 50 % | 40,000 | 35,000 | 25,000 | 21,000 |

Rationale for 95%-only: Kammuri 2019 reaches prob=0.917 (11/12) — a flat value across all
Region III thresholds from 2,000 to 33,500 houses. Excluding it at 80% would require a
threshold above 34,000 houses (implausible). The 95% level requires all 12 members to agree
(12/12 = 1.000), so Kammuri (11/12 = 0.917) is naturally excluded without special-casing.

## Key observations

- **Region II (Cagayan Valley) requires the highest thresholds** across all probability levels,
  driven by its large primary storm count (41 storms) — many storms are attributed here.
- **Region V (Bicol) and VIII (Eastern Visayas) have lower thresholds**, consistent with
  fewer primary storms (8 and 10 respectively) and lower expected damage per storm.
- **Winner-takes-all prevents double-counting**: without this constraint, storms like Phanfone
  would activate multiple regions; with it, each storm contributes to at most one region's count.
- **95%-only design creates a unanimous-agreement bar**: all 5 activated storms reach prob=1.000
  (12/12 members), making each activation maximally defensible.
- **Structural gap**: no combination of thresholds gives exactly 6 seasons from action alone —
  there is a jump from 5 seasons (RP 4.0 yr) to 7 seasons (RP 2.9 yr). The obs-rain trigger
  bridges this by adding Hagupit 2014.
