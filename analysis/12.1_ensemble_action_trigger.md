# 12.1 — Action Trigger: Ensemble Hindcast

Return-period analysis for the ensemble-based action trigger using hindcast
damage estimates for the four target regions (Regions II, III, V, VIII).

---

## Setup

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Data source | Ensemble hindcast — `ensemble_500increments_corrected/` |
| Record | 2006–2024 (19 years) |
| Storms | 79 unique storms |
| Damage thresholds | 500–40,000 houses (500-house increments, 80 levels) |
| Probability thresholds swept | 50 %, 60 %, 70 %, 80 %, 95 % |
| Regions | Region II (Cagayan Valley), III (Central Luzon), V (Bicol), VIII (Eastern Visayas) |
| Target RP | 3 years (6 seasons in 19 years) |

**Trigger logic**: a region activates in a season when the ensemble probability
of exceeding its damage threshold is ≥ the required probability. The trigger fires
when any one of the four regions activates.

---

## Calibrated thresholds at RP ≈ 3 yr (prob ≥ 50 %)

| Region | Damage threshold | Seasons activated | Return period (yr) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Region II (Cagayan Valley) | ≥ 8,000 houses | 6 | 3.2 |
| Region III (Central Luzon) | ≥ 5,000 houses | 6 | 3.2 |
| Region V (Bicol Region) | ≥ 3,000 houses | 6 | 3.2 |
| Region VIII (Eastern Visayas) | ≥ 4,000 houses | 6 | 3.2 |

All four regions calibrate to RP ≈ 3.2 yr at a 50 % probability threshold,
marginally above the 3-year target (6/19 = 3.17 yr).

---

## Return-period grid summary (prob ≥ 50 %)

The heatmap in the notebook sweeps required probability (50–95 %) against
damage threshold (1k–39k houses, every 2k) per region. Green-bordered cells
mark combinations within 0.4 yr of the 3-year target.

Key patterns across all regions:
- RP decreases rapidly below 3k–5k houses (trigger fires too often).
- RP rises steeply above 10k–15k houses (trigger becomes very rare).
- The 50 % probability threshold gives the most stable RP curve across the
  threshold range; stricter probability requirements (≥ 80 %) shift all RPs
  upward by 1–3 years.

---

## Key observations

- **All four regions calibrate consistently** — at prob ≥ 50 %, the 3-year RP
  is achieved with thresholds ranging from 3k (Region V) to 8k (Region II),
  reflecting regional differences in exposure and typhoon climatology.
- **Region V (Bicol) has the lowest damage threshold** to reach RP = 3 yr,
  consistent with its high landfall frequency and moisture exposure.
- **Region II (Cagayan Valley) requires the highest threshold** (8k), driven
  by fewer but more severe events (e.g. Carina 2024, Ulysses 2020).
- **Probability threshold sensitivity**: tightening from 50 % to 80 % adds
  roughly 1–2 years to the RP for the same damage threshold. The 50 % level
  is recommended to avoid over-conservatism given the 19-year record.
- The action trigger uses a **per-region composite** — the season is activated
  if any region crosses its individual threshold, not all four simultaneously.
